How it works

Stainless where it counts. Non-stick where it matters.

RIDGES

The raised metal peaks

An etched network of stainless-steel ridges sits proud of the surface — that's what gives the pan its grip on a piece of chicken skin or a scallop, the same way a cast-iron pan would.

VALLEYS

The non-stick channels

Between the ridges, a ceramic non-stick coating sits protected from direct utensil contact — so it keeps working, wash after wash, without flaking into your food.

RESULT

Stainless-level sear

You get the browning and fond that stainless steel is known for, without babysitting the pan the way stainless normally demands.

CLEAN-UP

Dishwasher, done

No seasoning rituals, no special oils. Soak briefly if something's really stuck, then it's dishwasher-safe like the rest of your kitchen.

Looking after it

Care & use

Seasoning

Warm a teaspoon of oil in the pan over medium-low heat for two to three minutes before first use. Performance improves slightly as it naturally re-seasons over time.

Heat guidance

This pan carries heat quickly — start lower than you would on a normal pan. Medium here behaves like medium-high on plain stainless.

Cleaning

Soak in warm soapy water if needed, then wipe or wash as normal. Dishwasher-safe for everyday use.
