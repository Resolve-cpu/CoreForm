Product Description

The CoreForm Pilates Board brings reformer style training into a board that folds flat in one second. It pairs an auto rebounding sliding track with detachable resistance handles and a thickened kneeling pad, so abs, back, balance and arm work all happen on the same piece of kit.

Built from thickened PP material with a 50% larger load bearing platform, it holds users up to 136kg (300 lbs). The non slip base keeps it steady through fast reps, and when the session is done it folds down to slide under a sofa or bed.

A built in digital timer sits in the console between the foot platforms, counting up or down so you can time sets without reaching for your phone.
